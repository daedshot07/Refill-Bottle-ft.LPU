CAMPUS SUSTAINABILITY

Small refills. Bigger impact.

Carrying a reusable water bottle across classes, hostels, and labs cuts down reliance on single-use plastic bottles across the university. Making existing water stations easier to locate removes friction from choosing a refill over packaged water.
